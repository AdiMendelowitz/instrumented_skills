Add a function parse_amount(text, decimal_sep='.') to money.py. It converts a billing string to a Decimal without rounding. With decimal_sep '.', a comma is a thousands separator, so '1,234.50' gives 1234.50. With decimal_sep ',', a full stop is the thousands separator and the comma is the decimal mark, so '1.234,50' gives 1234.50. Spaces of any kind, including non-breaking spaces and thin spaces, may appear as thousands separators in either form. Text wrapped in parentheses is negative, so '(12.50)' gives -12.50. A leading minus sign also works. Empty text, letters, two decimal marks, or a decimal_sep other than '.' or ',' raise ValueError. Leave format_amount unchanged and change only money.py.
